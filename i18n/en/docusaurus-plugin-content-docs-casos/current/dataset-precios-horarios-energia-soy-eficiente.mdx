# Hourly Electricity Prices by Tariff

*(Precios Horarios de Energía Eléctrica por Tarifa – Soy Eficiente)*

## Detailed description

This data product **centralizes, normalizes, and valorizes** complex Spanish electricity market price information from official sources (REE, OMIE). Through Empower-X, it provides access to a detailed historical dataset with hourly resolution, segmented by different access tariffs (from 2.0TD to 6.3TD) and broken down by geographical zones.

This use case acts as a single source of truth **(Trusted Data Provider)**, allowing energy retailers, energy managers, and prosumers to integrate this information into their billing simulations and procurement strategies. When combined with other datasets using Compute-to-Data capabilities, it facilitates the training of demand flexibility algorithms and automatic load shifting to off-peak hours, driving economic savings in a secure and interoperable environment.
