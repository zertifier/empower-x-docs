# Flexibility and PED – Rubí

*(PED Rubí Hourly Energy Dataset)*

## Dataset Purpose

This dataset acts as a **base data service** for algorithms used in the **PED Rubí** use case, aimed at the planning and operation of **Positive Energy Districts (PEDs)**, shared self-consumption, flexibility management, and 100% renewable charging.

The dataset provides an hourly and governed view of the energy behavior of urban supply points, enabling the execution of prediction algorithms, surplus optimization, simulation, and energy certification within a **Compute-to-Data** environment, without exposing raw data.

---

## Scope and Technical Considerations

Given the volume and criticality of urban energy data, the dataset **does not return complete historical records or unnecessary information**. Instead:

*   Data is **filtered by a specific time period** (day, week, or month)
*   The resolution is **hourly**
*   Only **strictly necessary fields** for authorized algorithms are included
*   Access is limited to authorized algorithmic executions

This design allows for efficient operation while complying with regulatory requirements for energy data.

---

## Dataset Type

*   **Private Dataset**
*   **Non-downloadable**
*   **Accessible only by authorized algorithms**
*   Governed under the common rules of the PED Rubí ecosystem
*   Executed via **Compute-to-Data on Empower-X**

Users do not access the data directly; only derived and aggregated results.

---

## Dataset Content

The dataset contains **hourly energy data** associated with urban electrical supply points, including public buildings, municipal assets, energy communities, and points with distributed generation.

Each record represents the energy behavior of **one supply point (CUPS) at a specific hour**.

---

## Dataset Format

The dataset follows a **fixed tabular format**, common and reusable throughout the ecosystem.

### Dataset Structure

| Field | Description |
| ----- | ----- |
| `cups_id` | Supply point identifier (anonymized if applicable) |
| `timestamp` | Date and time of the record (hourly resolution) |
| `energy_consumed_kwh` | Energy consumed in that hour (kWh) |
| `energy_generated_kwh` | Energy generated in that hour (kWh, if exists) |
| `energy_exported_kwh` | Energy exported to the grid in that hour (kWh, if exists) |

Generation and export fields may be null depending on the asset type.

---

## What Each Field Represents

*   **cups\_id**
    Uniquely identifies a supply point within the urban environment, preventing direct identification of holders or critical assets.

*   **timestamp**
    Allows for temporal analysis, demand prediction, scenario simulation, and flexibility coordination.

*   **energy\_consumed\_kwh**
    Energy demanded by the supply point during the indicated hour.

*   **energy\_generated\_kwh**
    Energy produced locally (e.g., municipal or community photovoltaic), when applicable.

*   **energy\_exported\_kwh**
    Energy surplus fed into the grid, relevant for shared self-consumption and PED optimization.

---

## Relation to Algorithms

This dataset feeds algorithms oriented towards:

*   **Hourly energy prediction**
*   **Flexibility management and aggregation**
*   **Optimization of shared self-consumption**
*   **PED scenario simulation**
*   **Calculation of sustainability indicators and certification**
*   **Energy audit and traceability**

Each algorithm accesses only the fields necessary for its function.

---

## Security, Governance, and Audit

*   The data **does not leave the secure environment**
*   Dataset download is not permitted
*   Access and executions controlled by governance policies
*   Use of audit and traceability mechanisms
*   Results always aggregated or derived

This approach is consistent with European requirements for data sovereignty, privacy, and energy regulation.

---

## Summary

The PED Rubí dataset provides a **structured, governed, and hourly view** of consumption, generation, and energy surpluses associated with urban supply points. Designed as a private data service for Compute-to-Data, it enables the execution of advanced algorithms for prediction, flexibility, simulation, and energy certification in the context of Positive Energy Districts, without exposing raw data or compromising confidentiality.
